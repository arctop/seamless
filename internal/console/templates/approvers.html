Add a Touch ID key

  
On a Mac, run seam touchid enroll in your own terminal. It installs the Seamless Approve app, which keeps its key in this Mac's Secure Enclave and shows the command that makes a one-time code for that key, run as the daemon's account. The key then approves from the agent's dialog with your finger, your Apple Watch or your login password. Enrolling the same Mac again replaces its key: the earlier one signs for its own removal and leaves this list with the enrollment. Another Mac's key, and a passkey, are never removed that way.
