Synced passkeys

Add it once

A passkey synced through iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or another password manager is one passkey: every device it syncs to signs in once it is approved, with nothing more to add. A device without it can sign in with your phone's passkey through the browser's QR prompt.

It works only for this server's name{{if .Origin}}, {{.Origin}}{{end}}. A passkey made for another site{{if not .Localhost}}, or for localhost on an install of its own,{{end}} does not work here, and renaming the server strands every passkey made for the old name.{{if .Localhost}} This console's passkeys belong to localhost, so only a browser on the server's own machine can make or use one.{{end}}
