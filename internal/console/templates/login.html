Passkeys

One passkey, every device it syncs to

A passkey synced through iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or another password manager is one passkey: add it once, and every device it syncs to signs in. A device without it can use your phone's passkey through the browser's QR prompt.

A passkey belongs to this server's name{{if .Origin}} ({{.Origin}}){{end}}: one made for another site, or for localhost on another install, does not work here, and renaming the server strands every passkey made for the old name.

The console's key no longer signs a browser in. The seam CLI still uses it.
